## Readout and probability mathematics

For ordered candidates i of question q, let `s_qi = sum_t log P_theta(c_qi,t | fixed prompt_q, c_qi,<t)` over the entire candidate continuation. Let `a_q = min argmax_i s_qi`, `t_q = 1{more than one i attains max s_q}`, and gold index `y_q`. Report `c_raw,q = 1{a_q=y_q}` and separately `c_R0,q = (1-t_q)c_raw,q`. Exact ties remain in every clean denominator. Report all n/correct/accuracy under both rules, source/type strata, gold-position counts, exact ties, inherited near-tie gap census at1e-3/1e-2/1e-1 nats, singleton/vacuity, duplicate candidate strings/tokens and probability-argmax mismatches. Singleton rows remain valid; invalid/missing rows make a panel incomplete rather than shrinking n. Near-tie scales are existing diagnostics, not eligibility thresholds.

At T=1 in CPU binary64, `m_q=max_i s_qi`, `Z_q=sum_i exp(s_qi-m_q)`, `p_qi=exp(s_qi-m_q)/Z_q`. Keep raw-score correctness even if binary64 probability first-argmax differs.

- `NLL = mean_q[log Z_q + (m_q-s_q,y_q)]` in nats, stable and unclipped; reject an unrepresentable/nonfinite value.
- `Brier_sum = mean_q sum_i (p_qi-1{i=y_q})^2`; no division by candidate count.
- `ECE10 = sum_b |sum_{q in b}(c_raw,q-max_i p_qi)|/n`. Edges are binary64 `i*0.1` for i=0..9 plus exactly1.0; bins are lower-inclusive, upper-exclusive, final upper-inclusive. Persist bin n, mean confidence, mean accuracy and contribution; empty means null and contribution zero.

Compute each model/seed and calibration/TEST suite separately using the exact supplement and conventions; no pooled seed/suite ECE or probability ensemble. Report NLL/Brier/ECE differences to both baselines per seed; fixed two-seed means average the two scalar metrics, never softmax vectors. NLL/Brier admit matched per-question loss differences if authenticated; ECE is an aggregate functional and has no per-question ECE loss. No new probability-loss confidence intervals or significance tests are introduced.
